{ Creative & Passionate }

UI/UX design Development Webmarketing
From front-end & UI/UX, toward marketing & communication

I design web experiences where craft and aesthetics are one and the same. A successful site never sacrifices one for the other. Every visual choice has a reason to exist, every line of code serves an intention. Today, I want to go further. Code and design taught me how to build, but I also want to learn how to bring what I create to life.

That's why I'm looking for a marketing and communications apprenticeship to accompany my master's. I want to understand how an interface becomes a brand, how a product finds its audience, how a story gets told beyond the screen.

My profile is multifaceted because my curiosity is too. Code, design, marketing, they all feed the same vision: create things that mean something and that move people. That's my strength. I'm still at the beginning of something bigger. Every project is another brick in a world I'm building my own way, and an invitation to build what comes next together.

This portfolio is only a glimpse of what's taking shape.
