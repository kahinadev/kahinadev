UI/UX Design 2 year apprenticeship
From front-end & UI/IX, toward marketing and communication

I design web experiences where technique and aesthetics are one. A successful website never sacrifices one for the other. Every visual choice has a purpose, every line of code serves an intention. Today, I want to go further. Code and design taught me how to build, but I also wan to learn how to bring what I create to life.

That's why I'm looking for a apprenticeship in marketing and communication for my master's degree. I want to understand how an interface becomes a brand, how a product finds its audience, how a story is told beyond the screen.

My profile is multifaceted because my curiosity is too. Code, design, marketing, it all feeds the same vision : creation things that have meaning and that move people. That's my strength. I'm still at the beginning of something bigger. Every project is another brick in a universe I'm building my own way, and an invitation to build what comes next together.

This portfolio is just a glimpse of what's taking shape.
