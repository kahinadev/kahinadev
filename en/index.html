UI/UX Design 2 year Apprenticeship
From front-end & UI/UX, toward marketing & communication

I design web experiences where technique and aesthetics are one. A successful site never sacrifices for the other. Every visual choice has a purpose, each line of code serves an intention. Today, I want to go further. Code and design have taught me how to build, but I also want to learn how to bring what I create to life.

That’s why I’m looking for an apprenticeship in marketing and communication for my master’s degree. I would like to understand how an interface becomes a brand, how a product finds its audience, how a story is told beyond the screen.

My profile is multiple because my curiosity is too. Code, design, marketing, all of this feeds the same vision: to create things that make sense and move people. It’s my strength. I’m still at the beginning of something bigger. Each project is one more brick in a universe that I build in my own way, and an invitation to build the sequel together.

This is just a glimpse of what is taking shape.
