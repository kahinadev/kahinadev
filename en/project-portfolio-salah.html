{ Portfolio Salah }

All Website Personal Portfolio

Context

Salah is looking for a 12 to 24 month community management apprenticeship, on a schedule of four days at the company and one in school. Since he doesn't yet have professional work to show, he needed a portfolio that presents his personnality, the way he tinks about social media, and his ability to imagine a brand from A to Z.
The portfolio therefore had to serve three purposes: introduce him clearly, show his tools, and highlight a first conceptual project, Maison Sélune, his future perfume brand. The goal is for recruiters to quickly understand who he is, what he can do, and how to reach him.

Art direction

I build the art direction around what he likes, namely clean, elegant and bright worlds, with very dark brown sections to create rhythm and depth as you scroll.

Process & tech stack

I built the site with Framer. At first, I wanted to create everything from scratch, starting with the structure and the three screen versions (desktop, tablet and mobile). To save time, I then chose to start from a template, which I fully adapted for the project.
Here are the diffrent steps:

Template: choosing template whose structure matches the desired style.
Rewriting the content: I rewrote the content in French for the introduction, background, tools and project copy.
Customization: I adapted the colors, typography and spacing to match the intended world.
Creating the pages: Home, About, Portfolio and the Maison Sélune projet page.
Responsive: checking that everything displays correctly across formats.
Testing and publishing: testing navigation and animations, then publishing.

Framer let me handle design, animations, responsiveness and publishing all in one place, in a fairly simple and fast way.
