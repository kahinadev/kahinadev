{ BntCook }

2026 Food Flyer Colorful

Context

This flyer was created for BntCook, the house of pastels, launched by a friend’s cousin.

Goal: : to highlight its Malian culinary know-how with a visual identity that reflects its origins.

On Sunday, Septembre 27, the flyer was dispatched, with the objective being to grab attention swiftly, featuring a design that was clear, inviting, and instantly recognizable, with the intent to promote easy order placement upon initial glance.

Art direction

The visual was made with warm colors and patterns inspired by Africa and Mali, to anchor the brand from its roots while remaining elegant and modern. The palette evokes the warmth and generosity of homemade cooking.
The logo organizes the design, whereas the open layout makes the menu, pricing, and pertinent details (pickup, delivery, WhatsApp ordering) easy to read at a single glance. The whole aims at a balance between cultural authenticity and commercial efficiency.

Results

The flyer was a success as soon as it was distributed on Sunday, September 27, 2026:
10 orders 270 pastels sold On Snapchat (my friend’s account): 387 views 188 spectators 11 screenshots 15 answers 349 interactions The other results from the other social networks are not available, as orders had to be stopped due to the influx.
