{ BntCook }

All Flyer Food

Context

This flyer was created for BNTCOOK, the house of pastels, launched by a friend's cousin. The idea was to showcase her Malian culinary expertise with a visual identity that reflects her roots while staying clean and understated.

Art direction

The visual uses warm colors and patterns inspired by Africa and Mali, anchoring the brand in its roots while keeping it elegant and mordern. The palette evokes the warmth and generosity of home cooking.
The logo structures the composition, while the airy layout makes the menu, prices and pratical details (pickup, delivery, WhatsApp orders) readable at a glance. The whole thing aims for a balance between cultural authenticity and commercial efficiency.

Results

The flyer was a success from the moment it went out on sunday, september 27, 2026:

10 orders 270 pastels sold

On snapchat (my friend's account):

387 views 188 sepctators 11 screenshots 15 answers 349 interactions

Other social media results aren't available, because orders had to be halted due to the surge in pastels sold.
